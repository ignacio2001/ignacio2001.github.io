PUSH — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · Developer: Ignacio Taylor · Contact: igtay2001@gmail.com

PUSH is a mobile game for iPhone made by Ignacio Taylor ("we"). This policy explains what data the game uses and the choices you have.

1. No account, no personal profile

The game has no login, no account and no game server. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and we do not run our own analytics.

2. Data stored only on your device

Your progress, scores, settings and local records (for example daily-challenge bests) are saved in a file on your device. This includes your records, your settings, whether you have seen the tutorial and the how-to-play screen, which special-block hints you have seen, the Zen game in progress (so it can resume) and your daily results. This data is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game may show ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC): a rewarded video you can choose to watch to continue a game, and an ad between games. When the device is online, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language, IP address used to derive an approximate location);
  • ad interaction data (ads shown, taps, completion of rewarded ads);
  • diagnostic data (crash and performance information related to ads);
  • the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Google uses this data to deliver, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud, and, where you consent, to show personalised ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland, certain US states and other regions where required): on first launch a consent message powered by Google's User Messaging Platform lets you accept, reject or manage choices. You can change them any time via Settings → PRIVACY OPTIONS in the game (the button is shown where the consent message applies).
  • Tracking (iOS): you can allow or deny tracking in the Apple prompt and later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Offline play: the game works fully without an internet connection; no ad data is exchanged while offline. The full game is also playable if you do not consent to personalised ads: the ad offers are then simply not shown.

5. Legal basis (GDPR)

Personalised advertising and use of device identifiers are based on your consent. Non-personalised ad delivery, fraud prevention and ad measurement rely on legitimate interests, as described by Google. You have the right to access, correct, delete or object to processing, and to complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). As we hold no personal data ourselves, requests about ad data are best directed to Google; we are happy to help — contact us at the email above.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and ad requests are limited to a maximum content rating of PG.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
